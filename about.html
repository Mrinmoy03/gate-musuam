About us

Gate City of the South

The city of Atlanta has called herself the “gate city of the south” for well over a century. The state of Georgia also has a magnificent and extraordinary history.

History

A Triumphal Arch for Atlanta

Can we tell Georgia's story to the world in a beautiful, engaging setting while also commemorating Atlanta for her incredible feats? With your support this became a reality.

It took 8 years to bring to you Atlanta's very own triumphal arch, housing a world-class boutique museum of Georgia history. Today the Millennium Gate stands proudly in the heart of the city, waiting to be explored for knowledge, art and entertainment.

2008

Museum Opening

Built to preserve and interpret Georgia history, art, and culture.

Our Mission

Preserving Georgia's Heritage

The Millennium Gate Museum's mission is to preserve and interpret Georgia history, art, culture and philanthropic heritage as well as highlight Georgia's historical and aesthetic relevance to the United States and to the world.

Architecture

Classically styled monumental arch located in Atlanta

As you first lay your eyes upon the museum, the emphasis on symbolic architecture becomes clear.

The distinct museum is housed in a classically styled monumental arch located in the center of Atlanta. It is designed in the tradition of classical Roman triumphal arches that have been built around the world over the past 2,500 years and houses a 12,000-square-foot museum that narrates Georgia’s history through sophisticated interactive technology, film, period rooms, and exhibitions.

Our Space

A Campus of History and Nature

In the heart of a bustling city, the museum's outdoor campus is just as inviting and educational as its indoor galleries.

The museum campus is dotted with monuments and statues each telling stories about brave men and women. The museum building, a work of art in itself, overlooks a pond and a pedestrian trail for guests to enjoy.

About The NMF

The National Monuments Foundation

The Millennium Gate Museum is a property of the National Monument Foundation. The National Monuments Foundation's mission is to create beautiful self-sustaining destination landmarks of national and historical relevance containing peace education centers for cultural, civic, and social activism for communities around the world.
