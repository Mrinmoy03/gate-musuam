Support & Learn

Help us tell our story

As a not-for-profit organization, we rely on the support of members, donors, corporate sponsors and partners, and volunteers to sustain our mission to connect people, history, and culture.

Donate now

Memberships

We offer 3 levels of membership which are listed below. For membership please contact rkerfoot@thegatemuseum.org or call (404) 881-0900

Individual
$50 /year
Unlimited free admission for member for 1 year
10% discount at the Museum gift shop
Advance invitation to The Gate Speaker Series events
Family
$100 /year
Unlimited free admission for 4 (2 adults and 2 children over the age of 8; children 8 and younger are free) for 1 year
10% discount at the Museum’s gift shop with presentation of membership card
Advance invitation to The Gate Speaker Series events
Benefactor
$200 /year
Unlimited free admission for up to 4 people for 1 year
15% discount at the Museum’s gift shop
Advance invitation to The Gate Speaker Series events for member and 1 guest
2 single-use guest passes per year
Personal tour of Museum by Executive Director of the Millennium Gate upon appointment for member and 1 guest once per year

More Ways to Support

Donate to the collection

Have something that could add to the museum? It may shine a new light on a part of Georgia's story. Please contact Richard Kerfoot (rkerfoot@thegatemuseum.org) to find out more about donating items to add to our collection.

Internship

We are proud to offer a robust internship program at the Museum. Interns gain valuable skills and insights as they get a first-hand view of the inner workings of our museum.
