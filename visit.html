Outdoor Space

The gardens around the Museum is a delight for visitors. Its lush pond—decorated by several varieties of plants—its stately trees, colorful shrubs and flowers and its meandering paths provide a stunning setting for some of the Museum's most important statues and monuments.

Rooftop Conservatory

Don't miss the spectacular views from the museum rooftop conservatory. You will see Atlanta's skyline all the way to the Mercedes Benz stadium, the pond reflecting the surrounding trees and witness the phenomenal growth of a city we call home.

Public Transportation

Access to the Millennium Gate Museum is centralized through MARTA's Arts Center Station, by means of MARTA rail and bus lines, as well as partner services Gwinnett County Transit, CobbLINC, and XpressGA.

There are three bus services from Arts Center Station that stop in Atlantic Station: MARTA bus lines 37 and 94, as well as a free shuttle service maintained by Atlantic Station itself.

For guests coming on Amtrak's Crescent Line, take MARTA bus line 110 from the Amtrak Station to Arts Center Station, then transfer to your bus line of choice for service to Atlantic Station.
