"The Many Lives of Andrew Young"

The Millennium Gate Museum was honored to showcase an exhibition on the life of Ambassador Andrew Young — a living legend and Civil Rights leader who rallied alongside Martin Luther King Jr., and a true American hero.

The exhibition was created by the museum's parent organization, the National Monuments Foundation in partnership with the Andrew Young Foundation. The museum had the privilege of hosting his 90th birthday celebration in March 2022 and was honored to house an exhibition based on his life's achievements.

Its narrative begins in New Orleans, LA, and follows Young across the nation and the world — drawn from the ambassador's own words, transcribed from interviews by AJC reporter Ernie Suggs, along with imagery from his papers collection. Chapters were reformatted and printed onto 30 fabric panels, which hung on the museum walls from March through October 2022 before touring the country.
