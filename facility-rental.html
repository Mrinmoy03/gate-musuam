Elegance in Every Detail

Designed in the tradition of classical Roman triumphal arches, the Millennium Gate Museum offers an elegant atmosphere in an extraordinary setting for your most significant moments.

A Memorable Backdrop

The Museum provides its guests an artistic and memorable backdrop for receptions, rehearsal dinners, Bar and Bat Mitzvah celebrations, corporate meetings, and other customized presentations or events.

Event staff will help you choose which of our event rental packages will best accommodate your needs.

Catering

Food and Beverages

Select a caterer from a list of Atlanta’s finest chefs and event professionals. Alcohol is permitted and must be provided by the event host, with bartending services handled by your chosen caterer.

Note: Outside caterers require a $750 fee and manual approval. restaurants are not considered caterers.

Productions

Filming & Photography

The Millennium Gate Museum is the perfect location for your film, commercial, and TV productions. Rates begin at $6,000 per day.

We also welcome proms, engagements, and family shoots starting at $100/hr. Proposals start at $500.
