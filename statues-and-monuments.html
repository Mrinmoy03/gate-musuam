Permanent Installations

Statues & Monuments

The museum houses a remarkable collection of bronze statues and stone monuments that celebrate pivotal figures and moments in global and local history.

01
Human Rights · 2023

Statue of Cyrus the Great

Commemorating the author of the world's first universal declaration of human rights, dedicated by Crown Prince Reza Pahlavi, son of the last Shah of Iran.

View in Detail →
02
Ancient Wonder

The Statue of Zeus at Olympia

A 3D-printed recreation of the lost wonder, originally made of wood, ivory, and gold — lost in a fire at the Palace of Lausus.

View in Detail →
03
Heroism · Stan Mullins

Hungarian Freedom Fighter

In honor of the fighters of the 1956 Hungarian Revolution, inspired by Erika Szeles, a 15-year-old nurse and freedom fighter.

View in Detail →
04
Colonial History

Chief Tomochichi

Co-founder of Georgia who welcomed General Oglethorpe and the original colonists with peace and diplomacy in 1733.

View in Detail →
05
Civic Virtue · A. Stoddart

Peace & Justice Gate

Classical bronzes of Dike (Justice) and Eirene (Peace) — daughters of Zeus and Themis — standing as eternal guardians of civic values.

View in Detail →
06
Literature & Liberty

Bust of José Martí

A tribute to the Apostle of Cuban Independence — poet, philosopher, and political theorist who united his nation.

View in Detail →
07
Vietnam Veterans

SGT Preston Tribble

Atlanta's Vietnam Veterans Memorial monuments and decorations, honoring those who served and the fallen.

View in Detail →
08
Presidential · Martin Dawe

Abraham Lincoln

Commemorating the 16th President. Georgia's role in his 1864 re-election was pivotal, secured by Sherman's Atlanta campaign.

View in Detail →
09
Founding Father · G. Kelley

George Washington

A bronze bust commemorating the first President's deep affinity for the state of Georgia and the city of Savannah.

View in Detail →
10
Featured Work

Heroic Spirit

An enlargement of the central figure of a monumental work by the august American sculptor Frederick Hart. Hart gifted this maquette to his friend Rodney Mims Cook, Jr. of the National Monuments Foundation, intending a proposed 14-foot work to be built in Atlanta.

Hart is most celebrated for the Vietnam Memorial soldier group at the Maya Lin wall, the National Cathedral central doorway tympanum, and President Jimmy Carter on the State Capitol Grounds in Atlanta.
